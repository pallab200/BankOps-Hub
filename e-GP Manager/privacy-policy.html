Privacy Policy for e-GP Auto Fetcher

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the e-GP Auto Fetcher (e-GP Manager) browser extension.

Summary: We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. All tender fetching and processing occurs entirely locally on your computer.

1. Information We Collect

The extension does not collect, track, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or user data to any external server or third party.

2. How the Extension Works

The extension acts strictly as a local utility tool:

  • It reads publicly available tender details (such as Tender ID, Ministry/Division, Procuring Entity, Document Fees, Tender Amount) from the Government e-GP portal (eprocure.gov.bd) only when requested by the user.
  • It passes this data locally to your Credit Commitment / e-GP Manager form running in your browser.
  • No data is uploaded to any remote server.

3. Permissions Used

  • scripting & tabs: Used solely to interact with the active e-GP tender notice tab and extract tender fields for local display.
  • cookies: Used only to maintain the user's existing logged-in session on the eprocure.gov.bd portal while fetching tender details.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, transfer, or disclose any user data to third parties under any circumstances.

5. Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this policy will be posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via your extension support contact.
